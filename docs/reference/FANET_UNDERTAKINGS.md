# FA-Net undertakings (club and authorised person)

Two blank UAE FA forms supplied by the owner, hand-summarised. **Not legal advice; the club's full agreement with UAE FA may contain more.**

## Club undertaking (2 pages: points 1 to 9, then Body, Designation, Name, Date, Signature, Stamp)
The club undertakes to:
1. abide by UAE FA regulations and circulars on registering local and foreign players, professional or amateur, and technical and administrative staff, **and the protection of minors**;
2. know and follow the e-registration procedures and give its staff the technical requirements;
3. **tell UAE FA who its employees in charge of e-transactions are, their responsibilities and the consequences; make sure they are trained; notify any change; and prevent use of the system by unauthorised personnel**;
4. **notify UAE FA of the people authorised to sign transactions and the form of their signatures**, and any change;
5. accept that application data is exchanged (club, player, intermediary, contract, staff, venue, board, score sheet data) and keep it **confidential**;
6. accept that FA-Net will check the club's technical and organisational measures;
7. accept that FA-Net contains **highly confidential data**, whose disclosure may cause severe damage, and that the club is fully responsible;
8. waive claims against FA-Net and UAE FA except for deliberate conduct or negligence;
9. be fully responsible for any non-compliance.

## Person acknowledgement (1 page: name, mobile, email, club, signature, date)
The person confirms they:
1. are trained and will enter and **follow up** the club's transactions;
2. will keep FA-Net information **strictly confidential, not disclose it to any third party or unauthorised person**, and not use it for personal or others' interest;
3. **will not disclose or transfer the password to anyone** and will guard it;
4. will not use the system after UAE FA dismisses them from the job and a replacement is recruited;
5. are liable for any failure to comply.

## What it means for the system
* The club keeps a **roster of FA-Net users and authorised signatories**: name, role, contact, signed acknowledgement, specimen signature, training, start and end dates. The platform keeps the signed forms and **reminds the club to notify UAE FA of any change**. Leaving staff lose access on their end date.
* **We never hold FA-Net passwords.** Any helper that touches FA-Net would have to work inside the person's own logged-in session.
* **FA-Net information must not go to third parties.** A tool that **sends documents from our platform to FA-Net** is a different thing from a tool that **reads FA-Net screens and copies data into our platform**. The second looks like disclosure to a third party. So: keep what we record from FA-Net to the minimum (submitted yes/no, date, optionally a reference), keep FA-Net-originated data out of the platform unless UAE FA or counsel confirms it is acceptable, and **ask UAE FA before any assisted upload**.
* The club and the people are responsible for how FA-Net is used. The platform should make it easy to stay inside these promises, not blur them.
