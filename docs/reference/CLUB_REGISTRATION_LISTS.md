# How clubs present registration requirements (2026/27)

Hand transcriptions of lists supplied by the owner. **Not official UAE FA text.** The authoritative UAE FA documents and the UAE FA medical form are on the owner's own computer and have not been seen yet. No personal data from sample documents is recorded here.

## The key finding
Clubs list **different things for the same federation**. Some of it is UAE FA's; some is the club's own add-on (forms, utility bills, translations, how to send). So the rules need **two layers**: a federation layer (maintained by us from UAE FA documents) and a club layer (each club switches add-ons on or off and adds its own).

What this means for the rules table:
* A requirement has: **holder** (player, father, mother, family), **alternatives** (any of), **language** (English, Arabic, both), **certified translation needed**, **colour copy**, **both sides**, **validity** (current, this season, 5 years), **issuer variants** (see school history below), **owner** (federation or club).
* The "Emirati player" shortcut (few documents) appears in two lists. It matches Fursan's "Local Player".

## School history is issuer-specific
The sample certificate is a **KHDA "Certificate of School Registration History"**: bilingual, lists each academic year with grade/year and school name for Dubai private schools, states that any later alteration makes it invalid. Only covers **Dubai private schools**. Another list says school history for the country comes from the **Ministry of Education** website. Other emirates' authorities were not seen. Treat "school history" as one requirement with issuer variants. Automatic checks: years covered (five consecutive), name and date of birth match, issuer, no alteration marks.

## UAE FA "Request for Approval of Amateur Minor Player" (called "Father adoption form" by Fursan)
Two pages, fillable, signatures required.
* **Parent declaration** (completed by the parents): reasons and date of the family's move to the UAE; the date the decision to relocate was made; why a parent did not move with the family, if so; circumstances and reasons for choosing the UAE. **If the player moved without a parent (case C), an additional signed statement authorising travel and residence, or official custody documents.** Parent signature.
* **Club declaration**: how and when the player and club first made contact; tick *First registration* or *International transfer*; declaration the player has not played for a first national team; confirmation that submitted documents are correct and acceptance of the **age determination protocol**; signature and **club stamp**.
* Both pages carry player name, date of birth, club. The form **changes by season** (sponsor logos in the footer), so templates must be versioned.

## Al Ittifaq FC academy terms and conditions (what a club's T&Cs contain)
Useful as a model of what a parent must accept: age phases (Discovery 4-7, Acquisition 8-12, Development 13-15); members may be asked to train with other age groups; health disclosure; required kit; drop-off and pick-up; transport; codes of conduct for members and parents; **fees: per-season registration fee at joining, termly or full-season billing, splitting via Tabby or Tamara**; kit entitlement differs for *Squad* and *Academy*; **cancellation with 1 month's written notice, medical credit only after more than 2 consecutive missed weeks with a certificate, valid 3 months, no make-up sessions**; **consent to school engagement** (opt out at enrolment); **consent to images and video for marketing**; liability waiver; law and courts of Dubai; signature fields. Shows the product needs: versioned club T&Cs, separate media consent, cancellation/credit rules as settings, programme types (Academy vs Squad), and BNPL providers as a later payment option.
