# How three clubs price 2026/27 (as supplied)

Hand summaries of three price lists and two training schedules from the owner. Amounts in AED. **The clubs' own documents, not verified.** Phone numbers and contact names on the sheets are not recorded here.

## Fursan Hispania ("UAE FA Players Registration Fees 2026-2027")
* Season August 2026 to June 2027. Prices **include VAT**. AED 600 extra for match kits.
* **U10 to U12 (2017 to 2015):** full season, 3 sessions a week, **13,900**. Per-term registration (Term 1 / Term 2): **8,000**.
* **U13 to U16 (2014 to 2011):** full season **14,950**. Per term **8,500**.
* **Two terms**, not three. Two terms cost more than the full season.
* Full season in installments: **60% at registration, 40% in December**. A term is paid in full; **pro rata after 4 weeks of the term**.
* **Sibling discount: 15% on the second child, 20% on the third.**
* Includes arrangements for the games, some added sessions, scouting and feedback. **No refunds, no make-up sessions, also on public holidays.** Schedule, times and location may change by week.

## G Reds FC ("Prices 2026/27")
* **Three terms of 14 weeks, AED 4,300 each.** **Registration fee 1,350, including the kits** (training kit, tracksuit, sports bag, red and white match kits, polo shirt, warm-up kit).
* "Prices do not include matches, tournaments and league fees." **One-time payment is required in the full term plan.**
* Weekly schedule: blocks **6:00 to 7:30 pm** and **7:30 to 9:00 pm**; **U12 A and U12 B**; an **Academy** group training twice a week; one venue (a campus in Dubai Academic City).

## An "Elite" league squad (information sheet)
* For outstanding players born 2017 to 2009, to join the team in the UAE League 2026/27. Training Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday; league matches Saturday or Sunday.
* **Training fee 10,000** (10 months, 40 weeks, 1 August to 30 May) **plus league registration fee 4,500 = 14,500.** The league fee covers player and team registration, training, home and away kits, match ambulance, match organisation and a match bus outside Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman.
* **Pay in two steps:** an initial booking of **2,500** (cash or bank transfer) with the **registration and initial nomination form**, within a set window, then the rest, which may be split through **Tabby or Tamara**. The sheet lists the second payment as 13,000, which does not add up with 14,500 less 2,500; likely a typo or financing cost (to ask).
* **A nomination is void** if the form and first installment are not completed by the deadline, or the second installment by the final date.
* The deadlines on the sheet fall in December 2025 and May 2026 for the 2026/27 season: **the nomination happens many months before the season**.

## What the three lists tell us about the model
| Variation | Seen at |
|---|---|
| Number of terms: 2, 3, or one season | Fursan 2; G Reds 3 of 14 weeks; Elite one season |
| Price depends on age band | Fursan (U10 to U12 vs U13 to U16) |
| Several fee lines: training, registration, kit, league | G Reds, Elite, Fursan (kit) |
| Full season cheaper than the sum of terms | Fursan |
| Installments (60/40; deposit then balance; Tabby or Tamara) | Fursan, Elite |
| Pro rata for joining mid-term | Fursan |
| Sibling discount by child order | Fursan |
| VAT included or not stated | Fursan |
| What is included (kits, ambulance, bus) varies | all three |
| Programme types: academy (training) vs league team (training + league costs) | G Reds Academy; Elite squad |
| Place hold with a deadline, void if missed | Elite |
| Different training blocks and A/B teams | G Reds vs Fursan |
