Sources supplied by the owner: **Annual Circular of the Registration Department**, **Annual Circular for the Competitions** (1st, 2nd, 3rd Division and age groups), **Competitions Regulations**. Hand-summarised for planning. **Not a substitute for the originals; verify before relying on any number.** Section numbers refer to the source.

## 1. Registration (Registration Circular)

**System and responsibility.** All requests go through **FA-Net**; the club is fully responsible for the type of request, the data and the accuracy of documents (2.1). Each club needs trained FA-Net users, updated each season; every request must be approved by a system user authorised by the Executive Director and/or General Secretary. Forms count only with the club's authorised signatures, which the club must notify to UAE FA. UAE FA may ask for any original document before or after approval. **FIFA TMS** is used for international transfers (ITC).

**Dates.**
* Re-registration lists: 13 July to 3 August 2026. An amateur player is dismissed from the club if not on any team list after that.
* Age-group teams short of their numbers may register players from 13 July 2026 to 31 March 2027.
* Shifting a player between Team A and Team B: until 15 September 2026.
* Periods start 07:30 on the first day and end 15:30 on the last day.
* Requests are reviewed within **5 working days**. No player or official may play without a valid card. Urgent requests cost AED 3,000 (working days) or 5,000 (holidays).
* No request is accepted while the club owes anything from earlier seasons (14.4). Fees deducted from the club's FA-Net balance, otherwise payable within 30 days.

There is **no U18, U5 or U6 team** at UAE FA. A club's own training groups (for example Fursan's U5/6 and U18) are not UAE FA teams. "U-number = 2027 minus birth year" holds for U7 to U17.

**Registering up or down (4.3, 4.4).** Players born September to December of the previous year may be registered in the team for the ages U10 to U14 **if the club does not field the direct higher age**. If a club does not field a team, it may register players in the next higher team, up to two levels, except one level for U7 to U11. Guardian consent is required for minors (4.6). Residents and Born-in-UAE players under 10 can register without FIFA approval and must re-register at 10 (4.5). Minors over 10 registering for the first time need approval from the FIFA sub-committee.

**Categories (4.1, 5).** Citizens, Sons of UAE Women, Passport Holders, Born in UAE, Residents, Foreigners. **Passport Holder** (per the owner): holds a UAE passport but has fewer privileges than a native national, because the person lacks the Family Book (Khulasat Al Qaid), which records genealogy; the passport is more like a travel document. Player type within each: **minor amateur, adult amateur, professional** (foreigners: adult amateur and professional only). Residents must hold a valid UAE ID, be born in or after 2006 (first registration), and not have played for another country's national A team.

Rules under the table:
* **Rows 17, 18, 19 are not compulsory if proof of residence of more than 5 years is submitted; then rows 20 and 21 must be attached.** This is the UAE FA logic behind Fursan's "Resident over 5 years / under 5 years" lists. It is a **choice of route**, not a separate category.
* For **U7, U8, U9** teams only documents 1, 2, 3, 5, 9 are required, for all categories.
* For minors in the Born-in-UAE and Resident categories, documents must be **legally translated into English by a certified translator**.
* A player born outside the UAE: birth certificate certified by the relevant authorities.
* An international transfer certificate (ITC) is compulsory for each player previously registered outside the UAE. Domestic transfers need a transfer document, NOC or committee decision.
* Proof of guardianship (court ruling) in the absence of the father. The minor-player form and the 5-year academic proof are as in Fursan's list; the **club is responsible for securing and entering the academic record**.

**Photo specification (card and registration).** Recent; **4 x 6 cm**; white background; club sports uniform, head uncovered; head and shoulders only; colour and clear; glasses only if sports medical glasses.

**Player's card.** Needs a photo if none for the season, copies of ID/passport per category, a player declaration (Resident and Born in UAE, certified by the club), and the complete medical results on the prescribed form.

* Staff must be **21 or older**. Coaches need a licence from the Technical Department. Technical and administrative staff may be registered for **at most two teams** (exceptions: Academy Technical Director, Sports Manager, Academy Supervisor, all medical staff).
* **Medical staff:** professional licence (Team Doctor, Physiotherapist, Paramedic, Nurse) from the Ministry of Health or the Abu Dhabi, Dubai or Sharjah health authority or the Dubai ambulance corporation. Psychologist, Nutritionist, Masseur, Sports Scientist: CV, attested qualifications, experience certificates, licence if any, all in English. **Team Doctor: ACLS and BLS; Physiotherapist, Paramedic, Nurse: BLS; valid two years; only in-person certificates count.** If employed elsewhere: no-objection letter, or a written undertaking by the club. **Temporary medical card** for 3 months, extendable 2 months, AED 10,000 and 20,000, only once per person.
* Staff categories and titles are listed in section 11.3 (for example Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Goalkeepers Coach, Team Manager, Team Doctor, Physiotherapist, Kit man).

### Medical examinations (10)
* "Pre-Competition Medical Assessment" (**PCMA**) is the medical form. **Form (1)**: first team, U23, U21, U19, U17. **Form (2)**: U16 down to U7. Forms must be complete and **in colour**; no card without them.
* Valid **1 June 2026 to the end of the season**.
* The extensive senior form is summarised in `PCMA_FORM_2026_27.md`. The owner calls it "Form 2"; the circular calls it Form (1). The youth form is still to be supplied.
* Abnormal PCMA results: notify the Sports Medicine Committee on the Medical Notification Form within **7 days**.
* Examinations outside the UAE need a request, an approved centre, English results, and repeating within 10 working days in the UAE (fee AED 5,000).

## 2. Competitions (Competitions Circular and Regulations)

**Team A and Team B (circular, fourth, 6 to 8).** A club may enter an age group with teams A and B. A player registered in A may not play in B and vice versa (AED 10,000 fine and disciplinary referral). A player may move between A and B only if he has **not played any match after 15 September 2026**. A second team pays the participation fee again.

**Who may play (circular, fourth).** For each age-group competition: players registered in that team, plus players born **one year younger (U13 and below), two years younger (U14 to U19) or up to three (U21, with a 16-year condition)**, plus **two players** born September to December of the year before the team's year (U10 to U14). Example, U12 (born 2015): registered U12 players, players born 2016, and two players born September to December 2014.

**Match rules for youth.** Start-list sizes, minimum starters and substitution opportunities vary by age (that table was not reliably read; take from the source); substitution opportunities; every team must play every player a minimum time (30 minutes in U13/U12, 25 in U11/U10, **AED 750 per player** otherwise); AED 750 per missing starter in U13 to U10 (fine); match durations and pitch sizes by age. Withdrawing from a scheduled match: AED 20,000 (first team) or **AED 10,000 (age groups)**.

**Bench (circular, eighth).** Age-group matches: 8 persons on the bench, all registered on the match list, with mandatory designations: **Head Coach; an Administrator, Manager or Supervisor; and medical staff (Doctor, Physiotherapist, Paramedic or Nurse)**. Divisions: 10 persons, with Doctor, Physiotherapist or Paramedic.

**Match list (regulations 16).** The team manager enters and approves the list in FA-Net **from 24 hours until 90 minutes before kick-off**: starters, substitutes, captain and goalkeeper, and the registered technical, administrative and medical staff. Every listed person must be **physically present at verification** (16.7). Clubs carry copies of cards in case the system fails. Only three listed members may enter the field of play, wearing accreditation cards.

**Kits (regulations 13).** Each team brings an **official and a reserve kit, for outfield players and for goalkeepers**, as entered in FA-Net. The **first party (home)** wears the official kit; the second party wears the official or reserve kit if colours differ, agreed with the match commissioner. The match kit must be selected in FA-Net **at least 48 hours before the match**. If colours clash and a team cannot or will not change, it loses 0-3 after 15 minutes.

**Numbers and names (regulations 14).** Numbers 1 to 99, **number 1 for the goalkeeper**. A player keeps the **same name and number for the whole season**. Changing an approved number costs **AED 750**. Number printed in English; names may be Arabic or English, approved in FA-Net; names mandatory from U14. The number must be the same on all attire as allocated in the match list.

**Host club duties (circular sixth, regulations 35).** The home club provides for each match: an **ambulance with a licensed crew (present one hour before kick-off)**, security, ball kids and flag bearers (present two hours before), stretcher bearers, a medical clinic and a doping control room where required. Numbers by competition: age groups U14 and above: 1 ambulance, 8 security, 4 ball kids; U13 and below: 1 ambulance, 6 security, 2 ball kids. No ambulance, or late by more than 20 minutes: the match can be ended 3-0 and **AED 10,000** fine. The security plan goes to UAE FA 72 hours before.

**Participation fees (circular, fifth).** U21, U19, U17, U16: 7,000; U15, U14: 6,000; U13, U12: 5,000; U11, U10: 4,000; U9, U8, U7: 3,000. Doubled if late. Paid to UAE FA's bank account; proof uploaded in FA-Net.

**What the owner said differs or adds** (reported, not in these documents): Team B may play in **DOFA** with lighter registration rules; professional divisions need more extensive medical arrangements. The documents do show heavier medical for senior teams (PCMA Form 1 up to U17, stricter bench and ambulance numbers for divisions).
