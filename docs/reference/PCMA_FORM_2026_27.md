# UAE FA PCMA form, season 2026/27 (senior / professional version)

Hand summary of the blank form supplied by the owner (24 pages, fillable). **Structure only; no clinical content is copied.** The owner calls it "Form 2, for professional players". The registration circular numbers it **Form (1)**: first team, U23, U21, U19, U17 (U17 exempt from the infectious-disease and vitamin D tests). The circular's **Form (2)**, for U16 down to U7, **has not been supplied yet**; academies mostly use that one. Separate forms exist for referees and are out of scope.

## Parts of the form and who signs
| Part | Content | Signed by |
|---|---|---|
| Cover page | Player name, club, **UAE FA ID number**, "I have undergone the pre-competition examination for 2026/2027", medical history, summary ticks for each part (eligible / play not recommended) | Player (signature, date) |
| Cardiac assessment | Player details, general and family history, cardiovascular examination, 12-lead resting ECG, echocardiography, summary, cardiac eligibility yes/no | **Examining cardiologist and institution** (name, address, phone, email, stamp, signature, date) |
| Orthopaedic and general physical examination | Height, weight, general examination, fasting blood results, injury and operation history, current complaints, spine, hip, groin, thigh and further joints | Examining physician |
| Infectious disease and vitamin D | Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, HIV, syphilis, vitamin D: laboratory results **attached** | Laboratory results (attachments) |
| Final sign-off | "Eligible for competitive football for 2026/2027" or "Play not recommended", remarks | **Club doctor** (name, stamp, signature), **club stamp**, date |

Rules on the form: **any erasure or amendment makes it invalid.** The circular adds: complete, in **colour**, valid 1 June 2026 to season end, abnormal results reported to UAE FA's Sports Medicine Committee within **7 days** on the Medical Notification Form.

## What this means for the system
* The form is a **multi-part, multi-signer document with attachments**, filled in by clinicians, not by parents. It fits the "forms and signatures" foundation block, with outside signers (outside contacts).
* **Clinical content stays out of our database.** Proposal: store the signed PDF in a restricted file store (medical-access only) and keep only **metadata**: season, date, which parts are signed and stamped, attachments present, the eligibility tick, and whether an abnormal result needs the 7-day notice. Reason: heart scans, blood tests and infectious-disease results about children are the most sensitive data the platform could hold.
* **Automatic checks** are about completeness, not medicine: every part present, signatures and stamps present, season correct, colour copy, attachments present, no visible erasures, eligibility box ticked.
* **A "play not recommended" or abnormal result** creates a task for the club doctor and a 7-day countdown for the notice to UAE FA.
* The form asks for the player's **UAE FA ID number**. A first-time registrant may not have one yet; the order of steps needs checking.
