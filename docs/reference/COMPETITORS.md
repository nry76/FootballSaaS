# Competitor notes

**Reliability:** the sites of both products were blocked from the working environment, so these notes come from search-result excerpts and from what the product owner said earlier. Not verified first-hand. Someone should open both products and check.

## ClassCard (classcardapp.com)
General class-management software for academies, studios, tutoring and music schools. **Not football-specific; no sign of any federation compliance.** Listed as available in the UAE.

Features seen in excerpts:
* Online booking page: choose which classes are bookable, seats, price; packages and bundles
* Attendance, including student self check-in; staff mobile app for the day's classes
* Progress tracking with grade scales and rubrics, photos and videos, report cards
* Invoices with payment links; memberships to automate billing; reconciles sessions attended
* Automated reminders to reduce no-shows; in-app chat; real-time reports
* Branded mobile app for the school
* **Zoho Books integration:** syncs invoices, payments and student records; matches payments; recurring invoices

## Statixa (statixa.app), per the product owner's original brief
Invite-only, iOS-only, no compliance or deadline tracking, fee module is "pay in person, staff confirms in the app". Not re-verified.

## What this means for us
* ClassCard is the model for **general operations** (booking, billing, progress). Copy the good ideas when needed, but that is not our reason to exist.
* Our reason to exist: **UAE football compliance made easy** (registration documents by player category, deadlines and lead times, medical fitness, match-day checks, fines avoided). Neither product appears to do this.
* Decision recorded in DECISIONS.md: focus first, breadth later.
