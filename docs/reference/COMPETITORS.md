# Competitor notes

**Reliability key:** *Checked* = read from the live site by Claude in this project (web access enabled). *Second-hand* = reported by the owner's own scan. Where the two disagree, it is flagged. Sites change; re-check before relying on any number.

## Statixa (statixa.app): closest direct competitor

*Checked:* attendance and player development ratings; direct coach-player messaging with a report button; match highlights; fee tracking (membership, academy, match); cross-club talent pools and leagues; identity verification for staff and players; parental consent for under-18s sent to the parent's own email before data is processed; account deletion with data removal; club analytics and financial reporting; **staff HR and compliance document tracking**. Roles: Player, Coach, Manager, Admin, Organiser. Org types: Club, Academy, Social Group, Talent Pool, League. **iOS only, invitation codes, no public sign-up.** Fees: "pay in person at the club, staff confirming receipt in the app"; full online card and wallet payment "planned for a future release". Pricing not shown.

*Correction to earlier notes:* Statixa **does** track staff compliance documents. What we found no trace of is **federation registration, league deadlines or match-day checks**. Say "no federation compliance", not "no compliance".

*Second-hand, not seen on the page:* no jersey-number management, no handling of a player in two age groups, no drill plans beyond ratings.

## ClassCard (classcardapp.com): generic class management

Not football-specific. Its solutions pages cover sports academies, swim schools, dance, gymnastics, martial arts, tennis and football academies with the same product.

*Checked (features page):* online booking page; drag-and-drop scheduling with repeats; attendance with self check-in; class comments with photos; resource sharing and course selling; grade books and report cards; memberships with automated billing; invoices with payment links; online forms for lead capture; **lead management with interaction logging and task reminders**; reporting dashboards; alerts and reminders; branded student portals on custom domains; branch management for multi-location; time-zone handling; role-based access; integrations.

*Checked (pricing page), possibly region- or date-specific:* Starter $99/month, Growth $199 (adds AI assistance, recurring billing and instalments, single-step automations, **Zoho and Xero integrations**), Business $349 (multi-step automation, AI chatbot, gift cards, SSO), Enterprise custom. **Unlimited students and staff on all tiers.** Branded iOS/Android apps are a $249/month add-on. Card fees 2.4% to 3.9% plus fees, by region. 7-day free trial.

**Conflict:** the owner's scan reported per-staff-seat pricing (Starter $100 with 100-student cap and 5 seats, Business $150, extra seats $20 to $30). The live page fetched here shows no seat or student caps. One is out of date or region-specific. **Do not choose our pricing unit from either until someone looks at the page directly.**

*Second-hand:* zero transaction fee claim, "37% checkout value increase, 1.6x renewals" (marketing claims, unverified), branded PWA.

## FANet.ae (UAE FA portal)
*Checked:* public page is a login form (English/Arabic), copyright UAE Football Association. Public terms page covers payment data and sanctions only and says **nothing about automated access, bots or third-party tools**. No public API documentation found. Registration screens are behind login and were not seen.

## What this means for us
* Our opening is unchanged: **UAE football registration compliance** (documents by player category, live status, correct files, deadlines) plus lead-to-registration in one flow. Neither product appears to do it.
* ClassCard shows the value of lead capture with task reminders, reminders, memberships and accounting integrations; those belong in later builds.
* Pricing unit (per player, per club, per staff seat) is still an open decision.
