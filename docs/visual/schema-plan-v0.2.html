Five ideas the whole design rests on

Every club is walled offEach record belongs to one club. The database itself refuses to link one club's data to another's.
Children are protected in the databaseUnder 18: no personal data is used until the parent confirms by email. This is enforced below the app, so a bug cannot skip it.
Few roles, many permissionsManager, Admin, Coach, Player, Parent. A person can hold several roles, so a small club's owner can be Manager and Admin.
Built for clubs worldwideCurrency, country, time zone, tax label and consent age are club settings. Default: AED, UAE, 18.
No rankings of childrenProgress is shown against the child's own past only. There is no leaderboard anywhere in the design.

Three rules explained

1. One jersey number for a player who plays in two squads

The player owns one number for the season. When Omar (U11) also plays U12, we show only numbers free in both squads, so the club buys one kit. Try it.

2. The parent-consent gate for under-18s

Click a stage to see what the system may hold about the child at that point.

Turning 18: the player is asked for their own consent 30 days ahead. If it is not given within the grace period, the profile is hidden again until they consent.

3. Deleting data

When a parent or adult asks to delete, there is a 7-day undo window. After that:

DataResult
Login, name, contact details, date of birthDeleted
Photos, ID images, documents, medical detailsDeleted, files removed
Ratings, drills, attendance, badgesDeleted
Message textReplaced with "[deleted]"; other people's side of the thread stays
Consent proofReduced to type, date and status; no email or IP
Payments, bills, refunds, invoicesKept 1 year, under a reference number instead of a name, then deleted
The deletion request and audit entries (no personal values)Kept 1 year

This is the wording the Terms and Privacy Policy will need. A lawyer must confirm whether 1 year is long enough for a club's tax and accounting duties.

Still to decide before any SQL

  • Grace period after a player turns 18. Proposed 30 days. Do parents lose access at 18 unless the player links them?
  • Deletion undo window. Proposed 7 days.
  • Can Admins see medical records and staff documents, or Manager only?
  • Shortcut for existing rosters. May staff invite a parent by email to register their child, or must every parent start it themselves?
  • One account per club. A parent with children at two clubs needs a different email for each club. Accepted?
  • Platform support access. Read-only, up to 7 days, only compliance, fees and analytics summaries. Right level?
  • Age groups. Does your league use single birth years (U11 = born 2015) or two-year bands? This decides how "playing up" is detected.
  • Age of consent by country. 18 is the default. Which other countries do you expect early on?
