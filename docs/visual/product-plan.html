Which UAE FA team, and which matches?

UAE FA separates registering a player from letting him play. Teams are by birth year: U-number is 2027 minus the birth year for U7 to U17; U19 covers 2008 and 2009. Training groups such as U18 or U5/6 are not UAE FA teams.

Staff registration: same engine, different table

Coaches, managers and medical staff register with UAE FA too. Staff must be 21 or older, coaches need a licence, and technical and administrative staff can be on at most two teams.

Medical role:

Photo helper

UAE FA photo: recent, 4 x 6 cm, white background, club sports uniform, head uncovered, head and shoulders only, colour and clear. Try one of your own photos. It is processed in your browser and goes nowhere.

In the product the cropped, correctly sized photo goes straight into the checklist. Face and shoulders framing and a background fix are the next step. Putting the club kit on a photo stays a club choice for later. Links to outside photo services are off by default, because the photo shows a child.

Nobody in the dark, and no restarts

The pain you described: parents do not know where they are, and one wrong upload restarts everything. Try it. Pick a document, then press a button. A rejection touches only that document. Everything already approved stays approved.

    What happens after the parent uploads

    • Automatic checks: format (PDF/JPG/PNG), readable, both sides of the Emirates ID, expiry dates, name and date of birth match.
    • A person approves or says exactly what to fix.
    • One download per child, files named the way the club wants (Robert.Passport.pdf).
    • Medical: the platform emails the assigned clinic; the clinic offers times through a one-time link; the parent picks one.
