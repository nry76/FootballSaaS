Step 1: the safe foundation

22 tables67 testsinvented test data onlyplan only: no SQL or code yet

A club exists, with staff, parents and children in it, and the database refuses everything it must refuse. A parent sends a short enquiry, confirms by an emailed link, accepts a place and gives consent. Each role sees only what it should. Settings come from a club default. Every change is audited. A family can ask to be deleted. No documents, schedule, messages or money yet: those sit on top later, through the same walls.

Written up in docs/STEP1_PLAN.md. Everything below is clickable; the outcomes shown are what the tests will prove, not a running system.

1. The tables

Tap a block. Every club-owned table carries a club label and links only to rows of the same club (composite keys), so a cross-club link cannot exist. Reference tables are the same for all clubs and read-only to the app.

Policies never hold rules themselves. They call four small functions: my_club() (which club, and is the account active), has_permission(), can_see_person() and data_level() (A, B or C, worked out and never stored, so it cannot go stale). Later steps change a function, not every policy.

2. Who sees what

Pick a role to light up its column. Orange cells mean only through a function: the app cannot touch that table directly.

3. Try to break in

Pick who you are and what you try. Each result names the lock that stops you and the test that proves it. Four locks, each independent: Wall (your club), Permission (your role), Relationship (your own child or yourself), Child gate (consent and stage).

4. The child gate: two data levels

Press the events in any order. The gate sits inside the tables, so it holds for a Manager and even for the database's owner. The enquiry's questions are collected at once; what stays locked is identity papers, photo and medical, until a place is offered and accepted. Payment is not needed to reach level 2.

      Who submits in FA-Net, and what the undertakings say

      Each coach submits the list of their own players. Some clubs hire an admin to do the registration. So "FA-Net submitter" is a permission with a scope.

      PersonCan doScope
      Coach with the permissionPrepare and submit the list for their own playersTheir own teams
      Hired adminCollect documents, review, submitWhole club
      Authorised signatorySigns club forms; name and specimen signature known to UAE FANamed people only
      The club keeps a roster of FA-Net users and signatories: name, role, signed acknowledgement, specimen signature, training, start and end dates. The system reminds the club to tell UAE FA about any change, and removes access when someone leaves.
      From the undertakings: FA-Net information is strictly confidential and must not go to third parties, and passwords are never shared. So we never hold FA-Net passwords, we record from FA-Net only the minimum (sent, date, optional reference), and anything that reads FA-Net screens or fills them in waits for UAE FA's permission.

      Which UAE FA team, and which matches?

      UAE FA separates registering a player from letting him play. Teams are by birth year: U-number is 2027 minus the birth year for U7 to U17; U19 covers 2008 and 2009. Training groups such as U18 or U5/6 are not UAE FA teams.

      Entering teams for UAE FA

      The club ticks which teams it fields. Nothing is blocked, and nobody is asked up front about a B team. Only if an age group already has a team does the system ask. Try adding U11, then U7.

      Staff registration: same engine, different table

      Coaches, managers and medical staff register with UAE FA too. Staff must be 21 or older, coaches need a licence, and technical and administrative staff can be on at most two teams.

      Medical role:

      The medical form (PCMA): the clinic's job, one upload

      The extensive senior form is 24 pages and several clinicians sign it: the player, a cardiologist, an examining physician, the labs, and the club doctor with the club stamp. The system does not fill it in, split it, or judge it. The youth form (U16 and below) has not arrived yet.

      Clinic completes and signs→Parent (or the clinic by link) uploads the whole signed copy→A person confirms it arrived→Stored privately
      We check: a readable colour PDF for the right season, plus a reviewer's tick list: stamped, signed, orthopaedic section signed. Later, an automatic check for the presence of stamps and signatures can plug in as a rule, once the vendor and privacy are vetted.
      We do not: read the medical content, judge whether it is true, or copy any result into the database. A signature cannot prove the signer is an orthopaedist. The UAE FA ID number field is optional.
