Nobody in the dark, and no restarts

The pain you described: parents do not know where they are, and one wrong upload restarts everything. Try it. Pick a document, then press a button. A rejection touches only that document. Everything already approved stays approved.

What the parent is told, and when

    Parent: full tracker + messagesCoach: read-only status, can nudgeManager: one queue

    Coaches do not upload registration documents. The parent uploads, the system checks instantly, a person confirms, and every change is recorded with who did it.

    Submit by 14 Oct. UAE FA takes 3-4 weeks, so we remind every 2 days.

    What happens after the parent uploads

    • Automatic checks: format (PDF/JPG/PNG), readable, both sides of the Emirates ID, expiry dates, name and date of birth match.
    • A person approves or says exactly what to fix.
    • One download per child, files named the way the club wants (Robert.Passport.pdf).
    • Medical: the platform emails the assigned clinic; the clinic offers times through a one-time link; the parent picks one.

    Where leads come from (social, search, website)

    Ad, post or search result→Club landing page (tagged with source)→Short enquiry form→Lead with its source→Confirmed = counted for that source
    • SEO: each club gets a public page that search engines can read (club name, ages, trial times, location).
    • SEM and social: you run the ads elsewhere. We give each campaign its own tagged link and page, and show which sources bring confirmed families.
    • Later: send the "confirmed" result back to the ad platforms. Never a child's details, only what a parent agreed to.
    • A cookie and tracking consent notice is needed on these pages.
